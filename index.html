About Yellowstone

Yellowstone National Park was established in 1872. It was the world’s first national park and is famous for its geysers, hot springs, forests, rivers, and wildlife.

Amazing Attractions

Old Faithful

A famous geyser that regularly erupts and shoots hot water high into the air.

Grand Canyon of Yellowstone

A beautiful canyon with steep cliffs, a dramatic river, and a spectacular waterfall.

Grand Prismatic Spring

A stunning hot spring known for its bright rainbow-like colors and vivid geothermal patterns.

Geysers and Hot Springs

Yellowstone has more geysers than anywhere else on Earth, and its hot springs display an incredible range of colors.

Winter Geyser Eruption

A powerful geyser sends hot water and steam high above the snowy ground.

Grand Prismatic from Above

An aerial view reveals the vivid blue center and colorful rings of this famous spring.

Wildlife of Yellowstone

Yellowstone is home to some of North America’s most iconic wild animals.

Grizzly Bears

Grizzly bears are powerful animals that live in Yellowstone’s forests and mountains.

American Bison

Bison are large mammals that roam across Yellowstone’s grasslands and valleys.

Gray Wolves

Wolves live in family groups called packs and help maintain the park’s natural ecosystem.
