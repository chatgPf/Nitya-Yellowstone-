About Yellowstone

Yellowstone National Park was established in 1872. It was the world's first national park. It is famous for its geysers, hot springs, forests, rivers, and wildlife.

Amazing Attractions

Old Faithful

A famous geyser that regularly erupts and shoots hot water into the air.

Grand Canyon of Yellowstone

A beautiful canyon with steep cliffs and a spectacular river and waterfall.

Grand Prismatic Spring

A stunning hot spring known for its bright rainbow-like colors.

Geysers and Hot Springs

Yellowstone has more geysers than anywhere else on Earth. Its hot springs have beautiful colors.

Winter Geyser Eruption

A powerful geyser sends hot water and steam high above the snowy ground.

Grand Prismatic from Above

An aerial view shows the vivid blue center and colorful rings of this famous hot spring.

Wildlife of Yellowstone

Yellowstone is home to many amazing wild animals.

Grizzly Bears

Grizzly bears are powerful animals that live in Yellowstone's forests and mountains.

American Bison

Bison are large mammals that roam across Yellowstone's grasslands.

Gray Wolves

Wolves live and hunt in family groups called packs.
