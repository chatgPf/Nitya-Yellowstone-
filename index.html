Yellowstone is a living masterpiece.

From the roar of Old Faithful to the quiet beauty of alpine forests, Yellowstone blends raw power and wild serenity in one unforgettable landscape.

Old Faithful

One of Earth’s most iconic geysers, erupting on schedule and thrilling visitors for generations.

Grand Prismatic

Vivid mineral pools create a surreal rainbow of color in one of the park’s most famous hot springs.

Grand Canyon

Golden canyon walls and dramatic river views reward hikers and photographers alike.

Wildlife in the wild.

Yellowstone is one of the best places on Earth to watch iconic species roaming freely across open landscapes.

Grizzly Bears

Massive and powerful, these bears are a symbol of Yellowstone’s untamed wilderness.

American Bison

Roaming the grasslands in herds, bison shape the landscape and inspire awe at every turn.

Gray Wolves

Elusive and intelligent, wolves live in packs and quietly define the rhythms of the ecosystem.

Essentials for your trip.

Smart planning makes a big difference when exploring one of the planet’s most dramatic landscapes.

⏰

Best Time to Go

Late spring through early fall delivers the warmest weather and best access to roads and trails.

🧭

What to Pack

Bring layers, sturdy shoes, binoculars, water, and plenty of patience for wildlife sightings.

📍

Must-See Stops

Old Faithful, Grand Prismatic, Yellowstone Lake, and the Grand Canyon of the Yellowstone are essential.
